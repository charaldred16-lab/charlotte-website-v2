# Ocado offers case study

## What the page is based on

The case study uses only the project details supplied by Charlotte:

- A product manager proposed a missed-offers page at checkout.
- Research showed that the checkout concept helped some customers who changed their mind.
- Many customers believed an offer was already complete when they added products.
- Checkout was therefore too late to prevent much of the confusion.
- Charlotte helped redirect the Discovery team towards clearer understanding earlier in the journey.
- The resulting direction supported improved customer satisfaction and fewer failed offer additions.

## Deliberate design decision

No screenshots, UI mock-ups, customer quotations, research documents, metrics or Ocado product artefacts have been invented.

The page creates visual structure through two editorial modules instead:

1. **Research changed the question** — compares the original framing with the research-informed framing.
2. **Moving the intervention earlier** — maps the original and research-informed intervention points against a simplified shopping journey.

These modules communicate Charlotte's reasoning. They are explanatory page design, not representations of an Ocado interface or research artefact.
